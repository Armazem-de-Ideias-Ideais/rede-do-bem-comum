# Consent invitation (EN)

For high-exposure initiatives. **They are NOT published.** Variables: `{{nome_iniciativa}}`, `{{cidade}}`, `{{link_convite}}`.

**Subject:** An invitation, only if and how you wish

Hello, {{nome_iniciativa}} team.

We came across your work in public sources and think it has a place on the Common Good Network, an open map of self-managed initiatives, mutual aid and the commons.

**You are not on the map.** We know that for some initiatives, appearing in public has a cost, so we publish nothing without your agreement. We keep only the initiative's name on a private, access-restricted list, so that we can send this invitation.

If you wish, the link below is **single-use**, and only you have it:

{{link_convite}}

Through it you can see exactly what would be shown and decide:

- to appear by city only ({{cidade}}), or by a wider region, or **with no position on the map**;
- what goes into the summary and the categories;
- to **decline**, with no reason needed. If you prefer, we also remove your name from our private list.

On safety: we show no street address and no names of people, and the position is always the city centre. If you do not reply, nothing is published. If the link expires, simply ask for another through the site's contact form. And if this channel is not safe or not the best way to discuss this, please ignore and delete this message; nothing will happen.

Warm wishes,
Rafael Reinehr, for the Common Good Network
