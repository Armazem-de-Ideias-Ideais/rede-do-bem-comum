# General invitation for networks and movements (EN)

Variables: `{{nome_iniciativa}}` (name of the network or movement), `{{link_ficha}}` (listing or landing page).

**Subject:** An invitation to the Common Good Network

Hello, {{nome_iniciativa}} team.

We are launching the Common Good Network, a free, open map that brings together self-managed initiatives, mutual aid and the commons around the world. No ads, open data, free code.

Each initiative has a listing, chooses how it appears and can leave whenever it wishes. The Network shows initiatives, not people: we keep no addresses and no lists of members.

For a network or movement like yours, it can be a way to present your local nodes and ease meetings, exchanges and practical support, without exposing anyone. You can also help spread the word among the initiatives you know, so that more people find each other.
